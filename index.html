Nikos GClient

Nikos GClient is a private, self-hosted mail client used by a single person, its owner. It is not a public service, it does not accept sign-ups, and it is not offered to anyone else.

With the owner's explicit consent it reads the owner's own Gmail inbox, sorts new messages into columns, suggests replies, and sends a reply only when the owner clicks “Send it”. Google Tasks and Google Calendar may be added later, again only for the owner's own account.
